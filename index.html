Scope & privacy

Only one living generation is identified by name; no living birth dates, addresses, or contact information are published. All historical relationships shown are drawn from the ongoing family research, not a completed genealogical proof.

Unconfirmed research leads

Unverified ancestry

Michael Woolery’s proposed parents

Henry Woolery (1756–about 1820) and Eunice Hahn/Hawn (1768–1819) are proposed as Michael’s parents. No reviewed historical record directly establishes these relationships. They are research leads, not confirmed ancestors.

Research lead

Marie's proposed Guetzko parents

A 1930 census lists “Mamie B. Getzko,” age 18, daughter of Emil and Hulda. This may be Marie B. Guetzko, but no direct parent-child document has yet been verified.

Emil Otto Guetzkow (born 22 Jul 1888, Carver County, MN) is indexed as a son of William Guetzkow and Caroline Ross. Hulda Louise Henning (10 Apr 1888 – 1 Jun 1946, Carver County) is indexed as a daughter of Emil Henning and Bertha Hennig. These individual records are not proof that they were Marie's parents.

Emil · Hulda

Research lead

Bertha Johnson's Norwegian origins

1930 and 1940 censuses say Norway, while 1920 says Sweden. A White Star Line passenger record lists Bertha Johnson, age 24, traveling from Kristiania toward New York in 1899; U.S. censuses instead report immigration in 1903. Same-person identity is unproven.

1899 candidate passenger

Research lead

Samuel Bigsby in England

A baptism associated with John Bigsby and Mary Bray gives a birth date of 12 May 1817 in Grundisburgh, Suffolk. The parent names match American evidence, but the age/identity discrepancy is unresolved.
