Reading this tree

Each indented generation shows a person's parents. Living bridge people are identified by name only; their dates, locations and records are intentionally omitted. Branches can be opened on a phone.

Confidence describes the family relationship, not certainty about every date or birthplace. “Index reviewed” means the record transcription was examined; it does not mean the original document was read.

Scope & privacy

Living bridge generations may be identified by name, but no living birth dates, ages, locations, addresses, occupations, documents or sensitive details are published. Detailed evidence for living relationships remains in private research notes.

Anyone with the public website link can view it. Deceased ancestors are shown with evidence notes and source links.
